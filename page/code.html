Foundational Objectives

In the coming quarter, we focus on the intersection of user agency and spatial organization. Our goal is to transform the traditional document into a tactile environment.

This shift requires a fundamental rethinking of the interface, moving away from "flat" design toward a layered, textural experience that respects the user's focus.
